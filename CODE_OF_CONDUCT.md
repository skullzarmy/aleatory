# Code of conduct

Be straight with people. Argue with the work, not the person who did it.

Most of what happens here is somebody saying a thing is wrong and somebody else
saying why it is not. That is the job, and it stays useful as long as it is
about the thing. "This loses a draft when the transaction aborts" is the
argument. "You clearly did not think" is not.

Nobody here is owed your time. A question answered badly is worse than one not
answered, and a review that never arrives is an answer too, so say so if you
are not going to get to something.

## What gets somebody removed

Harassment, following somebody around the project to keep at them, slurs,
sexual attention nobody asked for, posting private information, threats. Also
sustained bad faith: arguing to win rather than to get it right, after being
asked to stop.

Removal means having a pull request closed, being blocked from the repository,
or both. There is no formula. It is a judgement, made by whoever maintains this.

## Reporting

Message a maintainer on [Discord](https://discord.gg/3kFMF8gUxP), or use
GitHub's private reporting on the Security tab if it is serious enough that a
public message would make it worse.

It is read by the maintainer and decided by the maintainer. There is no
committee, no process, and no appeal beyond asking again with something new. A
project this size cannot pretend otherwise, and pretending would be its own
kind of dishonesty.

## Where this applies

The repository, its issues and pull requests, and the project's Discord. What
you do elsewhere is your business until it lands on somebody here.
